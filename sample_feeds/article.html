City bridge opens to pedestrians

By Maya Rao

After four years of construction, the new river crossing opened to walkers and cyclists on Saturday morning, connecting the eastern neighbourhoods to the central station.

The bridge carries a dedicated tram line, two traffic lanes and a wide pedestrian deck. City engineers say the central arch was assembled off site and floated into position last spring.

Local businesses welcomed the opening, although several residents raised concerns about noise from the new tram line. The council says monitoring will continue for six months.

An opening festival is planned for next weekend, with guided walks and a temporary market on the western approach.
